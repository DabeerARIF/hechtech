ENC + ANC: What Makes a Difference?

By HESH.TECH Editorial   •   Oct 12, 2026   •   5 min read

Noise is everywhere: traffic, crowded markets, office chatter, and the hum of a fan. If you have ever struggled to hear a call or enjoy your music, you already know why noise control matters. ANC and ENC help in different ways.

What is ANC?

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) focuses on what you hear. Microphones pick up outside sounds, and the earbuds use opposing sound waves to reduce steady background noise. This can make music or podcasts easier to enjoy at a comfortable volume on a commute or in a busy study spot.

What is ENC?

Environmental Noise Cancellation (ENC) focuses on what others hear. It processes microphone input to reduce surrounding noise during calls, helping your voice come through more clearly when you are outdoors or in a busy place.

Why choose both?

ANC and ENC solve different problems:

  • ANC reduces background sound for your listening experience.
  • ENC helps reduce background noise picked up during calls.

Having both can be useful if you listen to music and take calls throughout the day, whether you are travelling, working, or studying.

Who benefits most?

  • Commuters dealing with traffic and public-transport noise.
  • Students who want to focus in busy spaces.
  • Professionals who take frequent calls on the go.
  • Travellers looking for a quieter journey.

Final thoughts

Good sound is not only about bass and volume. When choosing earbuds, check how their noise-control features work, along with comfort, microphone quality, and battery life.
