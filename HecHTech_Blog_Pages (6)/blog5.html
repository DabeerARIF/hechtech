Bluetooth Versions: Does the Number Matter?

By HESH.TECH Editorial   •   5 min read

A newer Bluetooth version can bring improvements, but the number alone does not tell the whole story.

Bluetooth connects phones, headphones, speakers, watches and many other accessories. Newer versions can improve efficiency, reliability or supported features, depending on the hardware.

What should you know?

Both devices need to support a feature for you to benefit from it. A phone with a recent Bluetooth version may still connect to an older accessory, but advanced features may not be available.

Things worth checking

For most buyers, connection stability, codec support, range and battery life are more useful things to compare than the version number alone.

Our takeaway

Read the full specifications of both devices and look for the features you actually need, such as multipoint pairing or low-energy operation.
