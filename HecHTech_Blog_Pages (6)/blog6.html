Smart TV Buying Guide: What to Check First

By HESH.TECH Editorial   •   5 min read

Screen size, display technology and software all affect your viewing experience. Start with these basics.

Choose a screen size based on your room and viewing distance. A larger screen is not always better if it feels overwhelming in a small space.

What should you know?

Compare resolution, brightness, contrast and viewing angles. If you watch movies in a dark room, contrast may matter a lot. For a bright room, brightness and reflections deserve attention.

Things worth checking

Check the smart TV platform, app availability, update support and the ports you need for consoles or sound systems. HDMI features can matter for gaming devices.

Our takeaway

Set a budget, compare real-world reviews and check the warranty. The best TV is the one that fits your room and viewing habits.
