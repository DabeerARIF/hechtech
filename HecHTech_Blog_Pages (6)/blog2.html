ANC vs ENC: What Is the Difference?

By HESH.TECH Editorial   •   5 min read

Both technologies help with noise, but they solve different problems. Here is a simple guide to choosing the right one.

ANC stands for Active Noise Cancellation. It is designed to reduce unwanted background sound that you hear while listening, such as the steady rumble of an engine or an air conditioner.

What should you know?

ENC means Environmental Noise Cancellation. It is commonly used to make your voice clearer during calls by reducing surrounding noise picked up by the microphone.

Things worth checking

If your priority is a quieter listening experience, look for ANC. If you spend lots of time on calls in busy places, ENC can be helpful. Some earbuds include both, but performance varies by model.

Our takeaway

Check independent reviews and fit before buying. A good seal, comfortable earbuds and reliable microphones all contribute to a better experience.
